## 2. DETAILED VEHICLE SPECIFICATIONS & ARCHETYPES

### 1. HOOD BULL (HERO CAR) — "THE BULL TAKES THE BAG"
- **Tagline**: The standard all-around vehicle with balanced performance.
- **Attributes**:
  - Role: All-Rounder
  - Weight: Medium (3/5)
  - Speed: Medium (3/5)
  - Handling: Medium (3/5)
  - Impact: Medium (3/5)
  - Stability: High (4/5)
  - Difficulty: Easy
- **Anatomy & Breakdown**:
  - **Antenna & Contact Head**: Flexible carbon mast with glowing neon green ring/spherical contact head.
  - **Cockpit**: Open bumper-car cockpit, racing bucket seat, sport steering wheel with bull horn emblems.
  - **Money Display**: Dynamic digital hood/dash display showing bag balance (default `$85`).
  - **Body**: Emerald metallic green clearcoat, aerodynamic front nose with dual projector headlights & amber DRL.
  - **Bumper**: Heavy-duty wrap-around rubber impact cushion with neon green lower accent lip.
  - **Wheels**: 4x chunky bumper-car wheels with 5-spoke chrome rims and neon green lips.
  - **Chassis**: Dark graphite structural lower chassis with battery box and suspension mounts.

### 2. HOOD BEAR — "THE BEAR CRASHES HARD"
- **Tagline**: A heavy and durable bumper car designed for maximum impact. Slower but hits the hardest.
- **Attributes**:
  - Role: Heavy Brawler / Juggernaut
  - Weight: High (4/5)
  - Speed: Low (2/5)
  - Handling: Low-Medium (2/5)
  - Impact: Very High (5/5)
  - Stability: High (4/5)
  - Difficulty: Medium
- **Anatomy & Breakdown**:
  - **Antenna**: Reinforced thick mast with ruby red glowing contact head.
  - **Cockpit**: Reinforced roll cage with bear ear earplates, heavy-duty padded bucket seat.
  - **Money Display**: Dynamic display on front bumper/hood showing `$120`.
  - **Body**: Bulky, wide-body muscular panels in dark charcoal and crimson red with Bear Face crest.
  - **Bumper**: Extra-thick reinforced dual-tier rubber impact collar with pulsing red hazard glow.
  - **Wheels**: Oversized heavy-tread wheels.

### 3. TSLA RACER — "SPEED. TECHNOLOGY. FULL SEND."
- **Tagline**: A lightweight, high-speed bumper car with superior handling. Perfect for hit-and-run and quick maneuvers.
- **Attributes**:
  - Role: Speed Demon / Hit-and-Run
  - Weight: Low (2/5)
  - Speed: High (4/5)
  - Handling: High (4/5)
  - Impact: Low (2/5)
  - Stability: Medium (3/5)
  - Difficulty: Medium-Hard
- **Anatomy & Breakdown**:
  - **Antenna**: Ultra-thin high-tech antenna with electric cyan glowing ring contact head.
  - **Cockpit**: Minimalist cyber cockpit, yoke steering wheel, digital glass HUD visor.
  - **Money Display**: Crisp cyan digital ticker showing `$200`.
  - **Body**: Streamlined aerodynamic white composite shell with crimson red racing accents and Tech 'T' crest.
  - **Bumper**: Low-profile lightweight composite bumper with electric cyan neon ring.
  - **Wheels**: Aero-disc wheels with cyan rim lighting.

### 4. NVDA AI — "CALCULATE. PREDICT. DOMINATE."
- **Tagline**: A precision-engineered bumper car with excellent handling and stability. Great for skilled players.
- **Attributes**:
  - Role: Precision / Cornering Specialist
  - Weight: Medium (3/5)
  - Speed: High (4/5)
  - Handling: Very High (5/5)
  - Impact: Medium (3/5)
  - Stability: High (4/5)
  - Difficulty: Hard
- **Anatomy & Breakdown**:
  - **Antenna**: Segmented neural mast with glowing purple orb/node contact head.
  - **Cockpit**: Cybernetic cockpit with matrix grid HUD display and purple ambient glow.
  - **Money Display**: Matrix-style digital display showing `$150`.
  - **Body**: Stealth obsidian black faceted bodywork with radioactive lime green accents and stylized AI eye emblem.
  - **Bumper**: Hex-patterned composite bumper with deep purple neon channel.
  - **Wheels**: High-grip carbon composite wheels with purple/green accents.

### 5. GOLD WHALE — "HIGH RISK. HIGH REWARD."
- **Tagline**: A premium, high-performance bumper car with extreme stats. Fast and powerful but harder to control.
- **Attributes**:
  - Role: Glass Cannon / Chaos Gambler
  - Weight: Medium (3/5)
  - Speed: Very High (5/5)
  - Handling: Low (2/5)
  - Impact: Very High (4/5)
  - Stability: Low (2/5)
  - Difficulty: Expert
- **Anatomy & Breakdown**:
  - **Antenna**: Dual gold-plated masts with brilliant amber/yellow diamond contact heads.
  - **Cockpit**: Ultra-luxury quilted gold leather seat, gold-trimmed wheel, VIP champagne cooler prop.
  - **Money Display**: Oversized glittering gold LED display showing `$500`.
  - **Body**: Mirror-finish 24K gold chrome shell with black carbon fiber louvers and golden diamond crest.
  - **Bumper**: Heavy gilded bumper with warm golden neon halo.
  - **Wheels**: Gold multi-spoke custom rims with golden underglow.

## 4. CODE INTEGRATION & ARCHITECTURE

1. **Vehicle Type Registry & Config**:
   - `src/vehicles/VehicleTypes.ts` declaring types (`'hood_bull' | 'hood_bear' | 'tsla_racer' | 'nvda_ai' | 'gold_whale'`) and vehicle configs.
2. **Model Builders**:
   - `HoodBullModelBuilder.ts` (Bull Type)
   - `HoodBearModelBuilder.ts` (Bear Type)
   - `TslaRacerModelBuilder.ts` (Tech Type)
   - `NvdaAiModelBuilder.ts` (AI Type)
   - `GoldWhaleModelBuilder.ts` (Gold Type)
3. **Vehicle Factory**:
   - `VehicleFactory.ts` providing unified instantiating of any vehicle archetype for player and AI opponents.
4. **Dynamic Opponent Spawning**:
   - Update `OpponentManager.ts` to spawn opponents across the different vehicle archetypes with their matching physics, stats, and visual models.
